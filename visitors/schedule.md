# Schedule & Opening Hours

## Regular Opening Hours

| Day | Time | Type | Who |
|-----|------|------|-----|
| **Wednesday** | 13:00 – 17:00 | Young Maker Lab | Supervised sessions for young makers (under 18) |
| **Thursday** | 18:00 – 22:00 | Open Lab | Open for all makers and visitors |
| **Saturday** | 14:00 – 18:00 | Open Lab | Open for all makers and visitors |
| **Sunday** | 10:00 – 13:00 | Young Maker Lab | Supervised sessions for young makers (under 18) |

> During Open Lab hours, everyone is welcome to visit, use machines, or work on projects. No reservation needed.
{.is-info}

## Workshops

Workshops are announced on our social media and via the volunteer WhatsApp group. Registration via the [Stamhoofd webshop](https://shop.stamhoofd.be/hightechlab/).

Workshop price varies by type. **50% discount for students** with valid student card.

Volunteers attend workshops for free.

## Student Lab

| Day | Time | Price |
|-----|------|-------|
| Monday | By arrangement | €20 per team per day |
| Saturday (before deadlines) | Morning | €10 per team |

Student teams must register in advance and work under supervision.

## Holiday & Exceptions

The lab may be closed on public holidays or when no volunteers are available for a shift. Check our social media or WhatsApp for last-minute changes.
