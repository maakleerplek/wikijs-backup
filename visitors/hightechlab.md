Welcome to the **High Tech Lab**, a volunteer-run digital fabrication makerspace in Leuven, part of [Maakleerplek VZW](https://maakleerplek.be).

We have laser cutters, 3D printers, CNC routers, electronics equipment, and much more — open to everyone.

![The lab](https://maakleerplek.be/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/PXL_20240507_111113453.MP_-scaled.jpeg)
