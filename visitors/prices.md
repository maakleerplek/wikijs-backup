# Prices

## Machine Usage

| Machine | Rate | Minimum | Payment |
|---------|------|---------|---------|
| **Laser Cutter** (large or small) | €0.50 per minute | €5.00 | Payconiq |
| **3D Printer** (lab filament) | €0.10 per gram | €5.00 | Payconiq |
| **3D Printer** (own filament) | €1.00 per hour | — | Payconiq |
| **Other machines** | Free | — | — |

> Weigh your 3D print on the scale at the 3D print station. Time your laser cutter usage yourself. Pay via the Payconiq QR code on the wall.
{.is-info}

## Volunteer Credit

Active volunteers receive **€5/month** in machine credit (self-tracked, expires after 12 months).

## Workshops

Workshop prices vary by type. **50% student discount** with valid student card.

Volunteers attend all workshops for free.

## Payment

All payments via **Payconiq** — scan the QR code at the payment station or on the wall.
