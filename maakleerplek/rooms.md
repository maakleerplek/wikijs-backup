## How to Book

1. Create an Outlook event.
2. **Add a location** → search for the room name.
3. Check availability in Room Finder.
4. Fill in times → save/send.
5. Verify in the shared calendar.

To **cancel**: delete the event or remove the location. Verify in the shared calendar.

## Rules

- **No 1:1 online meetings in meeting rooms** — use the belcel or an atelier.
- Freely bookable: Pauka, Loft, Schouwzaal, Belcel.
- **Kantine:** ask Kobe first. External partners must rent via info@maakleerplek.be.
- Reservations always take priority over ad-hoc use.
- Structural recurring meetings are pre-blocked by Kobe.

## Schouwzaal Meeting System

- Your event must have `schouwzaal@rooms.maakleerplek.be` as location.
- The meeting appears on the TV and touchscreen.
- Start via touchscreen → "meeting starten".
- Share your screen by joining from your own computer. **Disable your computer mic/speakers.**
- TV off? Use the remote. Externals can't join? Organizer must admit them. Volume? Touchscreen control.
