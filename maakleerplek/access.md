# Access & Smart Locks

## Retractable Bollard (Stapelhuisstraat)

The site is **car-free**. Vehicles only for loading/unloading.

1. Get the badge from the keylocker at the white gate. Code from site coordination only — **never share with externals**.
2. Badge must never leave the area between keylocker and bollard (AirTag secured).
3. Drive close, scan badge on the red sensor until it beeps. Safe to drive when the red light blinks orange.
4. **Exiting:** no badge needed. The right bollard lowers automatically via the induction loop.

## Salto Smart Locks (Exterior Doors)

Two methods: **physical keytag** or **Salto KS app**.

### Physical Keytag

Limited supply. Site coordination decides who gets one. Requires signed agreement (liability for loss).

### Salto KS App (Most Common)

1. Your coordinator of your subplek requests access from site coordination.
2. You receive an invite link by email (valid 3 days).
3. Create a Salto account via the link.
4. Download the **Salto KS** app and log in.

### Opening / Closing a Door

Hold keytag or phone (press "digital key") against the black cylinder.
- **Orange → Green flash** = success. Turn the cylinder to open.
- **Red flash** = failed. Try again.
- **Closing / Locking:** To lock, follow the same procedure but turn the cylinder closed.
- Persistent issues: **ict@maakleerplek.be**

## Physical Keys

- Interior doors to subplekken: key in the keylocker of that subplek — contact subplek coordination.
- Technical rooms, storage, mailbox, padlocks: contact site coordination.
