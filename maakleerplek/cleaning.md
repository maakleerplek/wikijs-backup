# Cleaning

## External Cleaning Service (Wonen en Werken)

Weekly on **Tuesday mornings**. Keep desks, tables, and floors clear the night before.

**Areas cleaned:**
Sanitair, kitchenette, cowork, vergaderzalen, trappen en gangen, kantine, HTL (every 2 weeks), Herstelhub Textiel (every 2 weeks), Textiel Lab (every 2 weeks).

**Their scope:** floor cleaning, sanitary cleaning, empty table surfaces, cobwebs, replacing bin liners.

## Self-Cleaning Supplies

| Location | Available |
|---|---|
| Kitchenette | Cleaning products, brush, squeegee, dustpan, cloths, mops, buckets, vacuum |
| Gang (bruine deur) | Brush, squeegee, dustpan |
| Waskot in kantine | Mop, squeegee, brush, dustpan, buckets, kantine/kitchen-specific products |

## Consumables

| Location | Items |
|---|---|
| Kitchenette | Hand soap, bin bags, dish soap |
| Opslagkot achter sanitair kantine | Toilet paper, hand soap, paper hand towels |

## Big Clean (Grote Schoonmaak)

Twice yearly, organized with all cowork and subplek coordinators.

## Kantine

Follow the checklist posted in the kantine. Fill in the cleaning log after finishing.

Contact the site coordinator for missing items or questions.
