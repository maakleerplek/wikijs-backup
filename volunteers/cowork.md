# Cowork

## How It Works

Cowork spots are rented via the site coordinator (info@maakleerplek.be). Your organisation or you as an individual sign a contract for a period with a number of FTE cowork spots. An Excel indicates which time slots use which desk.

- **Full-time occupancy** by the same person/org: desk may remain permanently set up.
- **Other desks:** must be cleared after each workday.

## Facilities

- Sanitair and shower (for bike commuters)
- Kitchenette (fridge, microwave, croque machine, stove, dishwasher)
- Coffee and tea
- Internet
- Personal locker (code-changeable)
- Phone booth (belcel)
- Printer (office use only — no large print runs)
- Meeting rooms (Schouwzaal and Pauka) — available from 1.5 FTE cowork spot. See [Room Booking](/visitors/rooms).
