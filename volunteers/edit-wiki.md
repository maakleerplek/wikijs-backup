## What is this wiki for?

<div style="display: flex; align-items: center; background-color: #f0f7ff; padding: 15px; border-left: 4px solid #005cc5; border-radius: 4px; margin-bottom: 1em;">
  <img src="/purpose.jpg" alt="Wiki Purpose" style="width: 150px; height: auto; margin-right: 20px; flex-shrink: 0; border-radius: 4px;">
  <div>
    <p>Everything administration and maakleerplek-related belongs on the <a href="https://maakleerplek.sharepoint.com/">SharePoint</a>. However, visitors cannot access the SharePoint, and it is a bit clumsy to keep track of technical documentation there. Therefore, this wiki aims to target three groups:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Visitors:</strong> Answering questions such as: "How do I 3D print something?" or "How do I use the laser cutter?"</li>
      <li><strong>Volunteers:</strong> Everything relevant to the daily maintenance of the lab that is not administration-related. For example: what to do if part X of the 3D printer breaks. <em>(Example of what belongs on SharePoint: what to do if the stock of drinks is empty.)</em></li>
      <li><strong>IT Maintenance:</strong> Because we want future volunteers to be able to maintain the spaghetti code Claude made for us that keeps the IT services up and running, we document how the IT infrastructure is set up here.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>
