# Open Labs

Open Labs are public sessions where anyone can come use the lab. **No reservation needed.**

## Schedule

| Session | Day | Time | Audience |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open Lab | Thursday | 18:00–22:00 | Everyone |
| Open Lab | Saturday | 14:00–18:00 | Everyone |
| Young Maker Lab | Wednesday | 13:00–17:00 | Under 18 |
| Young Maker Lab | Sunday | 10:00–13:00 | Under 18 |

## Volunteer Duties

- Indicate availability on the shift list (Google Sheets). Add your name in the TROOPS tab with your machine familiarity.
- **Wear a nametag.**
- Greet visitors and ask if you can help.
- Register visitors on the tablet at the entrance. Or log total visitors/volunteers when leaving.
- You can work on your own projects, but **give priority to visitors**.
- You're not expected to know all machines — refer visitors to someone who does, or email hightechlab@maakleerplek.be.

## Machine Issues During Open Lab

- **Laser cutter:** scan the QR code on the machine and fill in the form.
- **3D printer:** email hightechlab@maakleerplek.be.

## Queue Rules

When there is high demand for machines:
1. If you're ready to go (no LightBurn adjustments needed), you have priority.
1. Avoid designing on the LC computer.
2. Sign up on the whiteboard queue.
3. Maximum **20 minutes per turn**.

## Thursday Kantine

On Thursdays the Buurtkantine bar is open. Note: Visitors can still purchase an HTL drink from our fridge, but should only consume it in HTL and not take it to the bar.

## Donations

Open Lab is free. Promote free donations from visitors.
