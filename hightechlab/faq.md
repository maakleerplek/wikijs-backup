# Frequently Asked Questions

## Visiting

**When can I visit?**
During Open Lab hours: Thursday 18:00–22:00 and Saturday 14:00–18:00. No appointment needed.

**Do I need to pay an entrance fee?**
No. Entrance is free. You only pay for machine usage and materials.

**Where are you located?**
Stapelhuisstraat 13-15, 3000 Leuven (Vaartkom area).

## Machines

**Can I use any machine?**
Most machines are freely available. The **laser cutter** and **CNC machines** require certification — attend a workshop first.

**Can I bring my own material?**
Yes. For 3D printing with your own filament, the rate is €1/hour. For the laser cutter, check with the volunteer on shift whether your material is safe to cut.

## Volunteering

**How do I become a volunteer?**
Contact us at hightechlab@maakleerplek.be or speak to a volunteer during Open Lab. You'll sign a volunteering agreement and receive door access via the Salto KS app.

**What's expected of volunteers?**
Approximately 4 hours per month: covering shifts, maintaining equipment, giving workshops, or contributing to a team. Attend the monthly volunteer meeting (last Tuesday of the month).

**What do I get as a volunteer?**
- Free access to all machines (€5/month credit for paid machines)
- Free workshop attendance
- Part of a community of makers

## Workshops

**How do I sign up for a workshop?**
Buy a ticket via our [Stamhoofd webshop](https://shop.stamhoofd.be/hightechlab/). Workshops are announced on social media and in the lab.

**I'm a student. Do I get a discount?**
Yes — 50% off all workshops with a valid student card.
