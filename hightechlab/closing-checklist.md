# Closing Checklist

> Also available as a printable poster: [Canva link](https://www.canva.com/design/DAHA3crg5mk/W9OvWebvKLBhYub8QhPopQ/edit)
{.is-info}

## 3D Printing Corner

- [ ] Turn off computer screens and put PCs in sleep mode.
- [ ] Check filament for loose ends — secure into the edges of the roll.
- [ ] Check printers for failed or failing prints. If found: stop, remove, clean the plate.
- [ ] Put loose rolls back on the rack.
- [ ] Move finished prints to the "finished 3D prints" box.

## Electronics

- [ ] Turn off soldering irons (careful — hot!).
- [ ] Place loose cables on the rack on the right.
- [ ] Return tools to their proper place.
- [ ] Clean up leftover electronics and put them in their drawer.
- [ ] Place electronics trash under the TV box for recycling.

## CNC & Drill

- [ ] Verify CNC is turned off (check the PC).
- [ ] Verify drill is off (check controls on the left).
- [ ] Confirm all drill and CNC tools are in the box.
- [ ] Ensure no tools are left inside the drill or CNC.

## Laser Cutter

- [ ] Check for material inside — throw away or put in the scrap bin.
- [ ] Put the computer in sleep mode.
- [ ] Remove the key and return it to the key container.
- [ ] Ensure the laser cutter is closed and the bed is empty.
- [ ] *(Optional)* Check scrap for wood pieces too small to keep.

## General

- [ ] Return all tools to the tool rack.
- [ ] If unsure where something goes, just make sure it's cleaned up and not in the way.
- [ ] Dispose of trash. Paper/carton → box on top of washing machine.
- [ ] If bags are full, take them outside to the white gate.
- [ ] Turn off lights for the basement and HTL (switches on the left in the kantine).
- [ ] Close the kantine door and **lock HTL using the Salto keys app**.
