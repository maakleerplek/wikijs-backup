# Workshops

## Public Workshops

Evening sessions, **19:00–22:00**, max 6 participants.

**50% student discount** with valid student card.

Volunteers attend all workshops for free — no registration needed.

### Registration

Buy your ticket via the [Stamhoofd webshop](https://shop.stamhoofd.be/hightechlab/). Workshops are announced on our social media and in the lab.

### Cancellation

Workshops are cancelled if no participants have registered **7 days before** the session.

### For Volunteers

- Check the workshop schedule and add your name to help. Target: two volunteers per session.
- Arrive **30 minutes early** to set up laptops and the lab.

### Flow

1. **Start:** introduce HTL and Maakleerplek, round-table introductions.
2. **During:** flexible format. Slides available on SharePoint.
3. **After:** participants receive an email with slides, an evaluation form, and a possible certification invite.
