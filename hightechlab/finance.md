## Approval Thresholds

| Amount | Approval required from |
|---|---|
| < €100 | HTL coordinator or designated contact for that area |
| ≥ €100 | HTL coordinator or financial contact (will check budget and obtain approval) |

Designated contacts may self-approve up to €100, but only for their own area/equipment.

## Buying in a Local Shop

1. **Get approval** (see thresholds above).
2. **At the shop:** request a VAT invoice (*BTW factuur*) **before** checkout. Provide the invoice address above. Pay with the MLP debit card or your personal card.
3. **Document:** scan the invoice to PDF/JPEG. Complete the [MLP purchase requisition form](https://maakleerplek.sharepoint.com/sites/HighTechLab/Shared%20Documents/HTL%20Purchase%20Requisition.xlsx) (includes invoice upload and banking details for reimbursement).
4. **Follow up:** unpack, store the purchase, dispose of packaging, and inform the financial contact.

## Placing an Order Online

1. **Get approval** (see thresholds above).
2. **Supplier requirements:** must issue a valid Belgian VAT invoice. Prefer Belgian suppliers, then EU. Amazon and AliExpress are discouraged.
3. **Contact the financial responsible** (anton-jan.bons@maakleerplek.be) to place the order. Orders must use the maakleerplek vzw name/address/VAT. Professional accounts exist at Amazon, Bol, Kippers Hobby — do not use other accounts.
4. **Payment options:**
   - On invoice → accounting@maakleerplek.be
   - MLP debit card
   - Personal card (invoice must be in maakleerplek vzw name)
5. **Document:** scan if needed. Complete the [MLP purchase requisition form](https://maakleerplek.sharepoint.com/sites/HighTechLab/Shared%20Documents/HTL%20Purchase%20Requisition.xlsx).
6. **Follow up:** track deliveries, unpack, store, inform financial contact.

## Steps Summary

1. Check with your subteam lead (see [Teams](/en/hightechlab/teams)). If unsure, ask the coordinator.
2. Email **anton-jan.bons@maakleerplek.be** with:
   - Exactly what items and how many
   - Suggested supplier (Belgian preferred)
   - Estimated cost
   - Whether you have subteam lead approval
3. Financial responsible places the order or gives instructions for a local purchase.
