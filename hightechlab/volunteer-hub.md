### Getting access to OnShape

1. Create a free account at [onshape.com](https://www.onshape.com)
2. Ask a team admin (currently Ruben) to add you to the **Maakleerplek-HTL** team
3. They go to **Enterprise Settings → Teams → Maakleerplek-HTL → Add users** and enter your email

Once added you'll get a notification email and can access all HTL designs via the [HTL Workspace](https://cad.onshape.com/documents/t/69c57f84c4bc5ed2b8ffc598).
