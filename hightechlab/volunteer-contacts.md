## How Volunteers Communicate

Volunteers coordinate via **WhatsApp**. There are dedicated group chats per team (machines, ICT, stock, etc.) and a general HTL group. For anything urgent or unclear, use the general group. If you are not yet in the groups, ask an existing volunteer to add you.
